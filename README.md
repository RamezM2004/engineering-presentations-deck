### 5. `05-Thermoelectric-Generators-TEGs/`
* **Title:** Thermoelectric Generators (TEGs): Solid-State Energy Harvesting and Direct Thermal Conversion
* **Course:** *Thermofluids / Applied Thermodynamics* — German Jordanian University
* **Topics:** 
  - Physics of thermoelectric conversion: Seebeck effect ($S = -\Delta V / \Delta T$), Peltier effect, and Thomson effect.
  - Dimensionless figure of merit $ZT = (S^2 \sigma / \kappa) T$ and semiconductor material selection ($Bi_2Te_3$, $PbTe$, and SiGe alloys).
  - Thermal-to-electrical energy conversion efficiency, thermal interface resistance, and heat sink design.
  - Industrial applications: Automotive exhaust waste heat recovery, aerospace radioisotope thermoelectric generators (RTGs), and self-powered IoT nodes.
* **Artifact:** [`Thermoelectric_Generators_TEGs.pdf`](05-Thermoelectric-Generators-TEGs/Thermoelectric_Generators_TEGs.pdf)
